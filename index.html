About Me

I'm Firomsa Ahmed, a creative technologist blending design, development, and community building. Based in Ethiopia, I'm passionate about crafting digital experiences that solve real problems — from student systems to brand identities.

My journey started with a curiosity for how things work, leading me to build custom software for schools, clinics, and local entrepreneurs. I believe in open knowledge: I've trained over 180 students in web development and system thinking through workshops and mentorship.

When I'm not coding, I'm exploring UI/UX concepts, contributing to open source, or helping the next generation of developers find their path.
